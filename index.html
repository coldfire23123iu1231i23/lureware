FAQ

Questions, answered.

How fast will I get my video?
Most short-form edits are delivered within 24–48 hours of receiving your footage. Larger ad productions and monthly plans have priority queues with even faster turnaround.
What do you need from me to start?
Just your raw footage or photos (via Google Drive, Dropbox, or a link) and a short brief — tone, references, and where it's going. If you're not sure, we'll jump on a quick call and shape it together.
Are revisions really unlimited?
Yes. We revise until you're genuinely happy with the result. We find most projects land after one or two rounds, but there's no cap.
What formats will I receive?
We deliver in every ratio you need — 9:16 vertical for Reels/TikTok/Shorts, 1:1 square, and 16:9 widescreen — plus any platform-specific specs for ads.
Can you match my existing brand style?
Absolutely. Share examples you like or your current content and your dedicated editor will build a style that stays consistent across every video.
Do you work with US businesses and time zones?
Yes — we work with brands and creators across the US and align our communication and delivery windows to US business hours.
